Install in 4 steps
  1. Tap Download the app. If Chrome warns about the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for Chrome, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. Play Protect may ask to scan — tap Scan app or Install anyway.
  4. Open IG Reach → Log in with Facebook / Instagram → pick your Instagram account.

Your Instagram must be a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page (Instagram app → Settings → Account type and tools).
