Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

IG Reach ("the app") helps an Instagram Business or Creator account understand its performance, compare itself with public business accounts, and publish content. This policy explains what the app accesses and how it is used.

What we access

  • Your Instagram professional account's profile, posts, Reels and insights, via Meta's official Instagram API, only after you log in with Facebook and approve access.
  • Public profile data and recent public posts of other Instagram Business/Creator accounts, via Meta's Business Discovery API, to build comparisons.
  • Media files and captions you choose to publish through the app.

How we use it

Only to show you analytics, comparisons and recommendations, and to publish content you explicitly submit. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage

Your Meta access token is kept in an encrypted, signed session cookie on your own device and expires within about 60 days. Analytics are fetched live and not stored in a database. Files you upload for publishing are held temporarily so Instagram can fetch them, then may be deleted at any time.

Your choices

You can log out at any time, and remove the app's access in Facebook → Settings → Business Integrations. To request deletion of any data, email the contact below; we will respond within 30 days.
